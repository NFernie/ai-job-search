\begin{itemize}

\item \textbf{Static reservoir models}: Petrel field- and regional-scale models, workflow automation, uncertainty analysis, seismic depth conversion, and static-to-dynamic handoff (tNavigator).

\item \textbf{Field development / drill-well}: factory-style tight-gas campaigns (20+ wells, including frac), horizontal oil and gas well planning, conventional wells; 100+ onshore wells overseen.

\item \textbf{Wellsite and geomechanics}: onshore and offshore mudlogging, coring, wireline, and reporting; MEMs, offset-well reviews, and frac reviews.

\item \textbf{Play types}: shallow marine, tight and conventional fluvial, shale, and fractured granite.

\end{itemize}
