\vspace{1pt}
\begin{itemize}

\item \textbf{Reservoir characterization / well log interpretation}: gamma ray, resistivity (including LWD mnemonics), cuttings, fluorescence, and formation tops in a common TVDSS frame.

\item \textbf{Field development, drill-well and wellwork support}: wellsite operations geology, then development geology on producing assets; horizontal-well target windows and geosteering replay.

\item \textbf{Subsurface mapping (well-based)}: Petrel export of LAS logs, ASCII surveys, tops, and McKinlay XYZ point-set surfaces. Not claimed as seismic mapping or full property-model builds.

\item \textbf{Integration with adjacent disciplines}: career arc through wellsite, geomechanics, and development geology; independent tool work that sits next to drilling decisions.

\item \textbf{Basin and structural context}: Cooper-Eromanga thermal history; first-author work on shear-zone reactivation.

\end{itemize}
