\lettercontent{I am applying for the Geoscientist role with ExxonMobil in Kuala Lumpur. I am a development geologist with an MPhil in Earth Sciences and more than seven years at Santos, spanning wellsite operations, geomechanics, and development geology on producing assets. I would put that well-based reservoir description to work for the global development and production teams supported from KL.}

\lettercontent{ExxonMobil's Kuala Lumpur Technology Center is one of the company's energy technology centres. Official pages describe it as a hub delivering IT and engineering expertise to Upstream, Product Solutions, and Low Carbon Solutions teams worldwide. That matches how I already work: well data first, then a recommendation drillers and reservoir engineers can use. I am based in Brisbane and ready to relocate.}

\lettercontent{Here is how I would take on the tasks in the posting:}

{\raggedright\fontspec[Path = OpenFonts/fonts/raleway/]{Raleway-Medium}\fontsize{11pt}{13pt}\selectfont
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Reservoir characterization from wells:} I interpret gamma ray, resistivity, cuttings, and fluorescence against formation tops and trajectories in a common TVDSS frame, including a public Stimpee 6 replay (45 of 90 stations in the McKinlay target window).
    \item \textbf{Drill-well support:} I started as a wellsite geologist, spent a year in geomechanics, and now work development geology on producing assets. The geosteering tool is independent of Santos. It is the method I would bring to well-planning discussions and to checking whether a horizontal stayed in the target window.
    \item \textbf{Structural analysis and integrated decisions:} I have published first-author work on shear-zone reactivation and Cooper-Eromanga thermal history, and I would sit with reservoir engineers by keeping logs, tops, and trajectories in one TVDSS frame. Quantitative seismic interpretation, sequence stratigraphy, volumetric assessment, and full static-model construction are not in my toolkit. I would grow those next to the geophysicists and geomodellers on the team rather than claim them here.
\end{itemize}\par}
\vspace{6pt}

\lettercontent{ExxonMobil has been in Malaysia since 1893. KLTC teams already work remotely with site teams in North America, Africa, Europe, the Middle East, and Asia. I would bring development-geology and well-log work into that same global support pattern, not treat this as a posting on the Malaysian PSC platforms.}
