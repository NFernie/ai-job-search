\lettercontent{Dear Hiring Manager,}

\lettercontent{I am applying for the Geoscientist role with ExxonMobil in Kuala Lumpur. I am a development geologist with an MPhil in Earth Sciences and more than seven years of upstream work at Santos on producing assets, and I want to do that work in a centre that supports global development teams rather than a single basin.}

\lettercontent{ExxonMobil's Kuala Lumpur Technology Center is one of the company's energy technology centres, built to give Upstream, Product Solutions, and Low Carbon Solutions teams worldwide a single engineering and geoscience hub. That matches how I already work: well data first, then a decision the drillers and reservoir engineers can use. I am based in Brisbane and ready to relocate.}

\lettercontent{Here is how I would take on the tasks in the posting:}

{\raggedright\fontspec[Path = OpenFonts/fonts/raleway/]{Raleway-Medium}\fontsize{11pt}{13pt}\selectfont
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Reservoir description from wells:} I interpret gamma ray, resistivity, cuttings, and fluorescence against formation tops and trajectories in a common TVDSS frame, including a public Stimpee 6 replay (45 of 90 stations in the McKinlay target window).
    \item \textbf{Drill-well / wellwork support:} I started as a wellsite geologist, spent a year in geomechanics, and now work development geology. The geosteering tool is independent of Santos, but it is the method I would bring to planning and post-execution review.
    \item \textbf{Honest gaps:} quantitative seismic interpretation, sequence stratigraphy, volumetric assessment, and full static-model construction are not in my daily toolkit yet. They sit next to the well-based mapping and Petrel surface export I already do, and I would close them inside an integrated team rather than claim them here.
\end{itemize}\par}

I want to be on that geoscience side of the hub.}
